# About Me
I am interested in using mathematical models to understand ecological dynamics and evolutionary processes in different biological systems. I initially focused on the evolutionary transition from free-living organisms to obligate symbiosis (parasitic and mutualistic included). My interest then expanded to studying ecological networks in the face of environmental change. My fascination with symbiosis (parasitic and mutualistic included) is now channeled into understanding the effect that these symbionts have on whole ecological networks and in the context of environmental change.
